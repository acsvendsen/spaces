How Spaces works

Add things

Pick an item in the library on the left, then click where it goes in the room. Pieces from the starting room are added once, in their modelled size; the other lamp designs are generic examples. Add from a shop link brings in a product: Spaces uses the shop’s own 3D model when the page has one and otherwise builds one from its photos.

Move and edit

Click an item to select it and drag to move it. Its settings open under Selected on the right, with exact measurements. Right-click an item for quick actions: switch a lamp, hide or remove. Room lists everything in the room; hide, show or remove pieces there, and choose the floor. Plan view makes positions easier to see. Wall lights snap to the chosen wall.

Lights

Every lamp has its own switch and dimmer under Lights, and its colour and output under Selected. The live view is lit by your lamps only, as the full render is: light and shadows come from each bulb, and a fabric shade glows with the light passing through it. Light bouncing off walls is spread evenly and only the four brightest lamps cast shadows, so the full render remains the reference. Work light lights the room evenly while you place things; it never appears in a render.

Saving and rendering

Demo edits stay in this browser. Sign in and save a private project to store your work with your Spaces account. Use Layout file to export a portable backup. Render this view asks the Spaces renderer for a full image of a saved private project. Private project edits save automatically to your account; images update only when you request a render. Jobs run one at a time, and finished images stay in My projects.

Groups

Use New group… under Room to choose pieces that move together, such as the TV arrangement. Click a grouped piece in the room to move its group, or select it in the list to edit it alone. Ungrouping keeps positions.

The room

Room 287 × 363 × 240 cm. Sofa 350 cm, TV bench 180 × 50 × 50 cm on 10 cm legs, 21 mm acoustic panels, LG G5 65″ and NSG10TY soundbar. Dublin upholstery follows the ILVA references in a darker Crush Forest bouclé; walls use Dyrup’s Dried Oregano and Country Soil swatches, both 02 matt. Paint and fabric colour, lamp output and beam spread are approximations; generic lamps are not manufacturer photometric models. The chaise projection, end-window position, frame trim and forward stove area are estimated.

Textures and photographed plants: Poly Haven, CC0. ILVA sofa reference. Dried Oregano · Country Soil. Fibrotech panel finishes and slat spacing are visual approximations.

Group items

Open product reference

Ready-made 3D models of this product

Found on Sketchfab, where modellers share them under licences that let Spaces use them with credit. Pick one only if it is the same product. Spaces resizes it to the shop's sizes and names its modeller wherever it shows the model.

On the shop page, save the main product photo (right-click it and choose Save Image) and add it here with the product's sizes.

Spaces uses the shop's own product photos. Check the sizes it found and add the missing ones.

Generation runs on the Spaces M1, with no cloud service, and takes 15 to 25 minutes. It fetches the shop's photos and builds the shape from the one that best shows the whole item on a plain background; the others are kept for a new attempt if you report the model. Flat items such as rugs and prints are built from the photo in seconds instead; their photo must show the whole item straight on. You can close this window and keep editing your room meanwhile; full renders and preparing other product links wait until it finishes. The result is an AI approximation of the photo: check it carefully before adding it.

Check the front, sides and back against the product reference. A correct size alone does not establish a correct shape or finish. A supplied 3D model is used as it is; a model generated from a photo is an AI approximation.

Report a wrong model

What is wrong?

The model is withdrawn from the shared library at once, so nobody else is given it; items already placed in a room stay until you remove them. Your report and the model's generation files are kept, so the cause can be found and fixed for every product. The generator itself is not retrained.

Sketchfab

Ready-made 3D models of many products are shared on Sketchfab. Sketchfab lets a model be downloaded only by someone with a free Sketchfab account, so Spaces uses your key when you choose one of those models. On Sketchfab, open Settings, then Password & API, copy your API token and paste it here.

Spaces keeps the key with your account, uses it only to download the models you choose, and never shows it again.
