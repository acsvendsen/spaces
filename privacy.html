Your information in Spaces

Updated 23 October 2026. Spaces is an invitation-only room-planning service operated by the person who invited you.

Sign-in

Google and Apple authenticate you on their own sites. Spaces does not receive your Google or Apple password. It stores the provider’s identifier for your account and your chosen Spaces username, so it can recognise you again. It does not use an email address to combine accounts, request your contacts, or access your Google Drive or iCloud files. Existing Spaces password accounts remain available; their passwords and recovery codes are stored as hashes.

Your private projects

Your saved room layouts, versions, uploaded reference images and finished renders are stored with your account on the operator’s private server. Other Spaces users cannot open them. The trusted server operator has administrative access; these records are not encrypted from that operator. Reference uploads do not request AI processing or publish your photos.

Shared furniture

Reusable product models and their public product information belong to a separate shared library. Your private rooms, placements, images and account identifiers are not copied into that library.

Browser and network data

Spaces uses secure cookies for your session and security checks. A short-lived additional cookie protects Google/Apple sign-in. Private drafts are kept in this browser tab and cleared on sign-out. Anonymous demo edits stay in this browser. The server keeps temporary keyed hashes of network addresses for abuse limits. The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages; signing in also connects to the provider you choose.

Backups and removal

Encrypted backups of server data are stored on the operator’s NAS. Backups can retain earlier copies after an item is removed. An automatic backup-retention policy and self-service permanent account deletion are not yet available. Contact the person who invited you for access, recovery or removal requests. Do not upload information you do not want the operator to hold.
